How I think

Five rules I build by.

  1. 01Reducing emotionally amplified mistakes beats maximizing returns. Show people where they are, not what will happen.
  2. 02A model that can't explain why it's wrong is more dangerous than no model at all.
  3. 03Regimes matter more than averages. A 2% move in a calm market is not the same as a 2% move in a volatile one.
  4. 04The hardest problems are rarely technical. They're about calibrating human judgment at scale without losing signal.
  5. 05If a tool doesn't change a decision, it's a dashboard. I build systems that force better choices under uncertainty.
