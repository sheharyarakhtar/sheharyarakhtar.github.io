How it works
  • Claude plans the shot list (front, back, side profile, close shots) and picks which reference images each shot needs.

How I think
  1. 01

    Reducing emotionally amplified mistakes beats maximizing returns. Show people where they are, not what will happen.

  2. 02

    A model that can't explain why it's wrong is more dangerous than no model at all.

  3. 03

    Regimes matter more than averages. A 2% move in a calm market is not the same as a 2% move in a volatile one.

  4. 04

    The hardest problems are rarely technical. They're about calibrating human judgment at scale without losing signal.

  5. 05

    If a tool doesn't change a decision, it's a dashboard. I build systems that force better choices under uncertainty.
